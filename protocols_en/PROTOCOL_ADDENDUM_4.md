# Addendum 4: Standard benchmarks (preregistered)

Written on 2026-10-12, before formal results were produced. The only thing done so far is the smoke test (intact model, 20 items per task), used to confirm the pipeline, parsing rules and baselines.

## Question
On six procedurally generated small tasks we saw: position-agnostic damages share one fragility ordering; more steps means more fragile; free generation is more fragile than forced choice. Do these still hold when switching to standard benchmarks?

## Model and damage
Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, settings as in the main protocol. Three position-agnostic damages, with the locked doses:
- NOISE: 0.20, 0.26, 0.31, 0.345, 0.375, 0.42, 5 seeds.
- QUANT: q = 15, 10, 8, 7, 6, 5.
- Random layer dropping ld_rand: n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 3 seeds.
61 conditions in total.

## Tasks
Already cached on the server, loaded offline. Shuffled with a fixed seed, then the first several items taken.

| Task | Source | Items | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSM8K | openai/gsm8k test set, items whose answer is an integer | 200 (COT uses the first 100) | GEN (give the number directly), CHOICE, COT (brief reasoning first, then answer, at most 384 tokens) |
| SCIQ | allenai/sciq test set, correct answer no more than 3 words | 200 | GEN, CHOICE, LETTER |
| ARC | ARC-Challenge test set, items with 4 options | 200 | GENOPT, CHOICE, LETTER |
| CSQA | CommonsenseQA validation set, 5 options | 200 | GENOPT, CHOICE, LETTER |
| BOOLQ | BoolQ validation set, passage no more than 150 words | 200 | GEN (answer yes or no), CHOICE |
| MMLU_CALC | MMLU high school mathematics and college physics, 100 items each | 200 | GENOPT, CHOICE, LETTER |
| MMLU_MEM | MMLU high school world history and nutrition, 100 items each | 200 | GENOPT, CHOICE, LETTER |
| Original tasks | ARITH1/2/3, FACT, RETR, the same 200 items as the main experiment | 200 each | GEN, CHOICE, LETTER |

Subject-verb agreement from the original tasks was removed (it behaved inconsistently across the two models, and on Mistral the forced-choice baseline was close to chance).

Format definitions:
- GEN: free generation, compared with the gold answer after parsing. Numbers must be exactly equal; for yes/no items take the first yes/no; for phrase items, after normalization the output must start with the gold answer.
- GENOPT: list the options with letters and require writing out the content of the correct option (not the letter); compared with the correct option after normalization. Used for option items whose answers are whole sentences.
- CHOICE: uses the same prompt as the corresponding generation format (option items use the GENOPT prompt, others use the GEN prompt), comparing the summed log-likelihood of each candidate answer as a continuation.
- LETTER: list the options and compare only the logits of the letter tokens.
- COT: reason briefly first, finally write "The answer is N.", and take the number there.

Chance level: 1/number of options for CHOICE, LETTER and GENOPT; 0.5 for the GEN of BOOLQ; 0 for the other GEN and COT.
The CHOICE distractors for GSM8K are generated by adding to/subtracting from or multiplying/dividing the gold answer by small amounts.

Adjustments made after the smoke test (all before formal results):
- The CHOICE for option items initially used a prompt without listed options; on the intact model ARC was 0.45 and MMLU 0.35–0.40, close to chance; after switching to share the prompt with GENOPT it was 0.70–1.00.
- The COT generation limit was raised from 256 to 384, and "brief" was added to the prompt, because at 256 the reasoning was often truncated.
- In the smoke test GSM8K direct answering was only 0.10 and is probably unusable; kept, handled by the threshold.

## Analysis
Same as before: corrected score, usability threshold (intact accuracy − chance level ≥ 0.3), D50 (noise and quantization by dose index, random layer dropping by number of layers), Spearman correlation. Noise and random layer dropping are averaged over seeds.
"Benchmark measures" means the usable measures from the seven benchmark tasks; "original measures" means the usable measures from the five original tasks.

## Predictions (written before the results)
- D1 reliability: split the 5 noise seeds in half; the median ordering correlation over the 10 splits on benchmark measures ≥ 0.8.
- D2 general ordering: on benchmark measures only, the ordering correlations of noise vs quantization, noise vs random layer dropping, and quantization vs random layer dropping are all ≥ 0.7.
- D3 format: for the same task, the D50 of the generation-type format (GEN or GENOPT) ≤ the D50 of CHOICE, holding in at least three quarters of the usable benchmark task × damage cells.
- D4 depth: COT and CHOICE of GSM8K are more fragile (smaller D50) than CHOICE of SCIQ, holding in at least 5 of the 6 comparisons over the three damages. Direct-answer GSM8K_GEN is also included if usable.
- D5 reproduction: the correlation between the noise ordering of the original measures in this batch and the noise ordering of the main experiment ≥ 0.9.
- D6 old-new consistency: combining benchmark measures and original measures, the ordering correlations between all three pairs of damages are still ≥ 0.7.
- Exploratory (no predictions): whether COT or direct answering is more fragile; the difference between the computation-leaning and memory-leaning MMLU groups; the behavior of the LETTER format.

## Stop rule
If fewer than 8 benchmark measures are usable, or fewer than 6 of them fall below 0.5 within the dose range (under any damage), report as "this set of doses or tasks cannot measure it", and do not add conditions to serve as the main result.

## Discrepancy with earlier statements
When reporting to the investigator, it was said that COT would be "run only at some doses". It was actually changed to run on all conditions but with only 100 items, which is simpler, and the results are a superset of the original statement.
